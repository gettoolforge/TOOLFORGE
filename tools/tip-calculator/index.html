Tipping quick guide (US norms)

  • Restaurant table service: 15–20% is standard; 20%+ for excellent service.
  • Takeaway/counter: usually 0–10%.
  • Taxi / delivery: ~10–15%; delivery often has a minimum (~€/$2–5).
  • In much of Europe service is included — tipping there is a small round-up, not an obligation.
