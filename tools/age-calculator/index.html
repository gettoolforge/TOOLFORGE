Age Calculator

Real calendar-correct age — not just days ÷ 365. Handles leap years and month lengths properly.


Why "days ÷ 365" is wrong

  • Leap years add a day roughly every 4 years — dividing by 365 drifts your age by weeks over a lifetime.
  • Months have 28–31 days, so "0.5 years" isn't a fixed number of months. This tool walks the actual calendar.
  • Legal ages (voting, retirement, contracts) are always defined in calendar terms — this matches how officials count.
