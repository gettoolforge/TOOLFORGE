What the numbers mean

  • Formula: EMI = P·i·(1+i)ⁿ / ((1+i)ⁿ − 1), i = monthly rate, n = months.
  • Early payments are mostly interest; the balance barely moves at first — that's amortization.
  • Paying even a little extra each month attacks the principal early and can cut years off the loan.
  • Mathematical estimate only — your lender's exact fees, insurance and rounding may differ.
