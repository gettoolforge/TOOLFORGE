Honest notes

  • Formula: Mifflin-St Jeor — the most accurate general BMR equation (~±10% for most people).
  • TDEE multiplies BMR by an activity factor that is necessarily approximate — track your weight for 2 weeks and adjust real intake based on what the scale does, not what any formula promises.
  • A ~250 kcal daily deficit ≈ −0.25 kg/week of fat; don't crash-diet below your BMR without medical supervision.
  • General information only, not medical advice.
