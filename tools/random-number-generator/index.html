Random Number Generator

Fair random numbers from your browser's cryptographic RNG — draws, dice, raffle winners.



What's it good for?

  • Raffles & giveaways: put winner numbers 1–N with "no repeats" on.
  • Dice: 1–6 per die; roll several for D&D-style sessions.
  • Sampling: pick test cases, review assignments, workout reps — anything where bias ruins the point.
  • Fairness comes from crypto.getRandomValues() — unpredictable OS-level randomness, not a looping math formula.
