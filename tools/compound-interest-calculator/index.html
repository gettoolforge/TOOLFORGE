The math (and the magic)

  • Monthly compounding formula: FV = P(1+i)ⁿ + PMT × [((1+i)ⁿ − 1)/i], where i = annual rate ÷ 12, n = months.
  • Time beats amount: €200/mo at 7% for 30 years ≈ €244k, of which only €72k is yours — the rest is interest on interest.
  • The "Rule of 72": at r% per year, money doubles in ≈ 72/r years. At 7% → ~10.3 years.
  • This shows mathematical growth, not financial advice — real returns vary and inflation shrinks future buying power.
