SRT Time Shift

Offset every cue by a fixed amount, or rescale all timings proportionally.

Common uses

  • Subtitles sync incorrectly after video re-encode or cut — shift everything at once.
  • Frame-rate conversion (23.976 ↔ 25 fps PAL speedup) — divide/multiply by exact ratio.
  • Negative values advance subtitles earlier.
