How it works (MET)

  • MET = Metabolic Equivalent of Task: 1 MET = energy cost of sitting still (~1 kcal/kg/hour).
  • Formula: kcal = MET × weight(kg) × hours. Brisk walking ≈ 4.3 MET, running 10 km/h ≈ 10 MET, cycling moderate ≈ 8 MET.
  • Estimates assume average fitness & terrain; fitness trackers may differ ±20%. Good enough to compare activities and set expectations.
