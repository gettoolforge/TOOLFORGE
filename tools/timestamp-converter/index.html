What is a Unix timestamp?

  • It's the number of seconds since 1 Jan 1970 00:00 UTC (the "epoch"). Computers use it everywhere: databases, APIs, logs.
  • 10 digits = seconds (until Nov 2286). 13 digits = milliseconds (JavaScript's Date.now()). This tool detects both automatically.
  • Timestamps are always UTC — your browser shows them in your local timezone above.

Common questions

  • Why is my converted time off by hours? It isn't — the timestamp is UTC; the display converts to your local timezone.
  • What happens in 2038? Old 32-bit systems overflow on 19 Jan 2038; modern 64-bit systems are fine until the year 292 billion.
