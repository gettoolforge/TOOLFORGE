Find & Replace

Bulk find-and-replace with regex power when you need it.

Regex examples

  • Delete blank lines: enable regex, find \n\s*\n replace \n\n
  • Swap first/last name: find (\w+) (\w+) replace $2 $1
  • Trim trailing spaces: find [ \t]+$ replace empty
  • Whole-words mode wraps your term in \b…\b so “cat” won’t hit “category”.
