Water Intake Calculator

A sensible daily hydration target based on weight, exercise and climate.

Honest guidance

  • Base rule of thumb: ~33 ml per kg bodyweight per day (≈2.3 L for 70 kg).
  • Add ~350–500 ml per 30 min of exercise, more in heat.
  • Food provides ~20% of fluid intake; thirst is a fine guide for most healthy people.
  • General info only — kidney or heart conditions need medical advice.
