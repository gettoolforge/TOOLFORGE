② is what percent of ?
answer = a ÷ b × 100
③ Change from to

Quick mental-math tricks

  • 10% of anything: move the decimal point one step left. 10% of 249 → 24.9
  • 5% = half of 10%. 15% = 10% + 5%.
  • x% of y = y% of x — 8% of 50 is easier as 50% of 8 (= 4).
  • A 20% discount then another 20% off ≠ 40% off — it's 36% (the second applies to the reduced price).
