How key detection works

  • The audio is decoded locally, then a chromagram is computed: an FFT-based histogram of which of the 12 pitch classes (C, C#, D…) dominate over time.
  • The chroma profile is correlated against Krumhansl-Kessler key profiles — psychoacoustic ratings of how strongly each scale degree anchors a key — across all 24 major and minor keys.
  • The best correlation wins; runner-up candidates are shown because relative keys (C major ↔ A minor) are genuinely close calls.
  • Detects better on harmonic material (chords, pads, melodic content) than on drums-only loops.
  • Mixing tip: keys that share a letter (C maj ↔ C min) or are adjacent on the circle of fifths blend most smoothly.
