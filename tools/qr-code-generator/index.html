QR Code Generator

Static QR codes generated entirely in your browser — they never expire because there's no redirect server involved.

Why these QR codes never expire

  • Many "free" QR generators create dynamic codes — the QR contains a short link to their server, which forwards to your destination. Stop paying, code dies.
  • These are static codes: your actual URL/content is encoded directly into the pattern. No middleman exists, so nothing can expire or be switched off.
  • Trade-off: static codes can't track scans and can't be edited after printing — generate again if content changes.
  • Generation happens locally with JavaScript — the data you encode never leaves your device.
  • Tip: keep content short (URLs under ~150 chars scan most reliably); longer data = denser pattern = harder scanning.
