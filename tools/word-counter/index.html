Common questions about word counts

  • How long is 500 words? About one page single-spaced (roughly 250–300 words per page). An average speaker reads ~150 words per minute aloud.
  • Typical limits: tweets/posts 280 chars · meta descriptions ~155 chars · essays often 500–1,000 words · blog posts 800–2,500 words.
  • Do spaces count as characters? Usually yes in platform limits — that's why both counts are shown above.

Why use this counter?

  • Counts as you type — no button needed, no waiting.
  • Your text never leaves your device. This page is plain JavaScript running locally; nothing is uploaded anywhere.
  • No limits, no account, no watermarks — count a sentence or a whole book chapter.
