Color Palette from Image

Drop in any image — extract its dominant colors for design inspiration. Analyzed locally via canvas.

How extraction works

  • The image is drawn to a canvas and sampled — every Nth pixel is bucketed into coarse color bins.
  • The most populated bins become the palette (median-ish representative per bin), sorted by dominance.
  • Similar colors are merged so you get distinct hues rather than ten near-identical shades.
  • Your image never leaves the device — canvas processing is fully local.
  • Click any swatch to copy its HEX value.
