JWT Decoder

Paste a JWT — see header, payload and expiry decoded. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Safety notes

  • Decoding ≠ verifying. The signature is only checked by whoever holds the secret key. This tool decodes; it does not prove authenticity.
  • Never paste production tokens into tools you don't trust — this one runs fully offline in your tab, but the habit matters.
  • exp (expiry) and iat (issued-at) are Unix seconds — shown here as dates.
