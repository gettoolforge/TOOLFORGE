One Rep Max Calculator

Enter a weight and the reps you managed — get your estimated 1RM and a training-percentage table.

The formulas

  • Epley: w × (1 + reps/30) — the most common general-purpose estimate.
  • Brzycki: w × 36/(37 − reps) — tends to read slightly lower at higher reps.
  • Lombardi: w × reps^0.10.
  • All formulas are estimates that drift above ~10 reps; test true 1RMs safely with a spotter.
  • %-table: use 80–90% for strength blocks, 65–75% for hypertrophy volume work.
