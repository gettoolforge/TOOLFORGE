Resize images & video without distortion — set the ratio, change one side, get the other.



Common aspect ratios

  • 16:9 — YouTube, TV, monitors (1920×1080, 1280×720)
  • 9:16 — Shorts, Reels, TikTok, phone screens (1080×1920)
  • 1:1 — Instagram feed posts (1080×1080)
  • 4:5 — Instagram portrait (feeds taller than wide)
  • 21:9 / 2.39:1 — ultrawide monitors & cinematic films
  • Tip: to keep sharpness when downscaling video, keep both sides even (divisible by 2) for most codecs.
