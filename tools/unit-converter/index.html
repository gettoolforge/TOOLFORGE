Handy reference points

  • 1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly · 1 mile = 1.609 km · 1 kg = 2.205 lb
  • °F → °C: subtract 32, multiply by 5/9 (e.g. 100°F ≈ 37.8°C)
  • Data: manufacturers count 1 GB = 1000 MB; Windows shows 1 GiB = 1024 MiB — that's why drives "shrink"
