How it works

  • One sleep cycle ≈ 90 minutes: light → deep → REM. Waking mid-cycle (deep phase) causes that groggy "hit by a truck" feeling.
  • This tool steps in full cycles from your target time and subtracts the minutes it takes you to fall asleep.
  • Adults do best with 5–6 cycles (7½–9 h). Fewer than 4 cycles regularly = accumulating sleep debt.
  • Cycle length varies person to person (~80–100 min) — treat this as a starting point, then adjust by how you feel.
  • General information only — not medical advice for sleep disorders.
