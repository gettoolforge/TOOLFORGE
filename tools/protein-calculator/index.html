The science ranges

  • Sedentary minimum: ~0.8 g/kg (RDA — a floor, not a target).
  • Muscle building: 1.6–2.2 g/kg is the evidence-backed sweet spot.
  • Fat loss phases: higher protein (1.8–2.7 g/kg) preserves lean mass.
  • Spread over 3–5 meals of 25–40 g each for best synthesis. Kidney-healthy people handle high protein fine; kidney conditions need medical advice.
