Honest limits of BMI

  • BMI = weight ÷ height². It's a screening number for populations, not a diagnosis of individuals.
  • It can't tell muscle from fat — athletes often score "overweight" while being perfectly healthy.
  • The same BMI means different health risk at different ages and ethnicities (WHO notes higher risk thresholds for some Asian populations).
  • Use it as one signal among several — waist size, blood work and how you feel matter more.
