VTT to SRT Converter

Convert WebVTT subtitles to the universally-supported SubRip format — paste or drop a file.

What gets converted

  • Timestamps: 00:00:01.000 --> becomes 00:00:01,000 --> (comma milliseconds) with sequential cue numbering added.
  • Cue settings like align:start line:0% are dropped (SRT doesn't support them).
  • Voice tags like <v Roger> are stripped cleanly.
  • NOTE blocks and the WEBVTT header are removed.
  • Basic <b><i><u> styling tags are kept (most SRT players render them).
