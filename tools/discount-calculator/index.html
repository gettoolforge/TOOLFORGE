Discount math that saves real money

  • Sale price = price × (1 − discount/100). Savings = price × discount/100.
  • Stacked discounts multiply, never add: −30% then −20% = ×0.70 ×0.80 = 56% of original → total −44%, not −50%. Stores know "extra 20% off" sounds better than it is.
  • To compare offers, ignore percentages and compare final prices — that's the only number that leaves your wallet.
